From Discovery To Certification

NL-FL Proving Workflow

NL-Prover develops and recovers mathematical routes, while FL-Prover turns validated derivations into machine-checkable Lean 4 proofs. The detailed workflow follows five connected stages.

  1. Problem Understanding and Route Exploration
  2. Proof Development and Bottleneck Identification
  3. Mechanism Blockage and Strategic Recovery
  4. Computation Certification and Proof Assembly
  5. Lean 4 Formalization and Verification
